Mu-sixk

Drop in your audio. It measures the sound and tells you what to fix, and by how much.

The file is

Drop a song here WAV, MP3, FLAC, M4A or OGG. It is analysed on this computer and never uploaded.
Drop the separate tracks here Vocal, kick, bass, keys and so on, each exported from the start of the song. Name the files after the instrument and the roles fill themselves in.

Teach it the sound you are going for

The built-in styles are general starting points. Give it finished songs you want yours to sit next to, and it measures their tonal balance, loudness and punch, then judges your mixes against that. Three to five songs from the same style works well. Everything is stored in this browser only.

Move learned sounds to another computer

Copy the text below, or paste text copied from another computer and press Import.

Measurements follow ITU-R BS.1770 (LUFS, true peak). Tone targets and the ranges for vocals, drums, bass and instrument levels are measured from 144 real, professionally mixed songs and their separate tracks (MUSDB18 excerpts). Treat every number as a place to start, then trust your ears.
