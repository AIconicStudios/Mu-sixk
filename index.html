Teach it the sound you are going for

The built-in styles are general starting points. Give it finished songs you want yours to sit next to, and it measures their tonal balance, loudness and punch, then judges your mixes against that. Three to five songs from the same style works well. Everything is stored in this browser only.

Move learned sounds to another computer

Copy the text below, or paste text copied from another computer and press Import.

Measurements follow ITU-R BS.1770 (LUFS, true peak). Tone targets, stem ranges and the vocal, drum, bass and instrument level model come from 144 real, professionally mixed songs and their separate tracks (MUSDB18 excerpts). Level estimates are typically within 1.6 dB (vocals) to 2.6 dB (bass). Treat every number as a place to start, then trust your ears.
